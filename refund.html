Refund policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Try first, then buy. The free trial is the full app, with no time limit and no limit on panoramas; only the «zenpano · trial» watermark differs. Because you can test everything before paying, and the key is made for your device and sent right away, licences are not refunded for a change of mind.

Check before you buy

Please make a few panoramas with the trial, on the same device you will use, from your own drone and photos. If the result is good with the watermark, it will be the same without it.

EU right of withdrawal

Consumers in the EU normally have 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase. For digital content supplied right away, that right ends once supply starts, if you agreed to it. At checkout you tick a box to ask for your key to be delivered immediately and to confirm that you then lose the right of withdrawal; we confirm this in the e-mail with your key.

When we do refund

  • The key does not work on the device it was made for, and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time.
  • zenpano does not do what the website describes for a drone listed as supported (not «test» or «beta»), and we cannot fix it.
  • You paid twice for the same thing by mistake: we refund the duplicate.
  • Any other case where the law of your country gives you a right to a refund. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

Write to sebipmobil@gmail.com with your order number (it starts with «txn_» and is in the receipt). Our reseller Paddle.com processes the refund to the card or account you paid with; the time it takes to appear depends on your bank. After a refund the licence ends; please remove the key from zenpano.

Not a refund case, but we help

  • Wrong device code at checkout: write to us with the right code and we send a new key, free.
  • Changed your phone or PC: write to us with the new device code; the new key is free, within reasonable limits.
