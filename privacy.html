Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Short version: zenpano works on your phone or PC. Your photos and panoramas never leave your device. We only receive what is needed to sell you a licence and send you the key, and we don't sell or share it for advertising.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Sebastian Nelu Pintea, Baia Mare, Romania, the author of zenpano. Contact: sebipmobil@gmail.com.

The apps

  • Photos and panoramas are processed only on your device. The apps do not upload them anywhere.
  • Android: the app does not ask for internet access at all.
  • Windows: the app goes online only when you press «Check for updates» in Help. It then asks GitHub for the number of the latest version; GitHub sees your IP address, as with any web request. Nothing about you or your photos is sent.
  • Licence check: it works offline. The device code is computed on your device from an identifier of the system and does not reveal that identifier.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking code is built into the apps.

When you buy a licence

Payments are handled by Paddle.com, our reseller and merchant of record. Paddle collects your payment and billing details and processes them as a controller under its own privacy policy (paddle.com/legal/privacy). We never see your card details.

From Paddle we receive, for each order:

  • your e-mail address and country;
  • the product, price, tax and order number;
  • the device code(s) you entered at checkout.

We use them to make and send your licence key, to send a new key if you change your device, to handle refunds and support, and to keep the records required by law. The legal basis is the contract with you and our legal obligations (GDPR art. 6(1)(b) and (c)).

When you write to us

If you e-mail us or use the feedback form, we receive what you send (for example your e-mail address, your drone model, sample photo sets) and use it only to answer and to improve zenpano. The feedback form is a Google Form, so Google processes it under its own privacy policy. Photo sets you share are used only for testing zenpano and are not published without your permission.

How long we keep it

  • Order and licence records (e-mail, country, device code, order number): for as long as you may need a new key, and at least as long as accounting and tax law requires.
  • E-mails and feedback: as long as needed to answer and follow up, then deleted.

Sharing and transfers

We don't sell your data and don't share it for advertising. We share it only with the services above, as far as they need it, and with authorities when the law requires. Some of these services are based outside the EU (for example in the United States); they rely on the safeguards the GDPR provides for such transfers.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. Write to sebipmobil@gmail.com. Some records we must keep by law even if you ask us to delete them. You can also complain to a data protection authority; in Romania this is ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If the change affects how we use data you already gave us, we will tell you by e-mail.
